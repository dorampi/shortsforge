ShortsForge

A personal desktop tool for making short vertical videos.

ShortsForge runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It assembles short vertical (9:16) video clips from footage the owner supplies, and can upload the finished clip to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

It is a single-user tool. There is no hosted service, no public sign-up, and no server that other people connect to.
